Delivery Information

We offer reliable delivery across Canada for custom promotional products, corporate gifts and printed merchandise. Delivery times vary depending on the product, quantity, customization requirements and delivery location.

Production & Processing Time

  • Production begins after your artwork and digital proof have been approved.
  • Customized and printed products require production time before dispatch.
  • Production time varies by product type, quantity and customization requirements.
  • Large or bulk orders may require additional production time.

Shipping Across Canada

  • Delivery is available to locations across Canada.
  • Shipping time depends on the delivery address and available shipping service.
  • Remote and regional locations may require additional transit time.
  • Orders containing multiple products may be dispatched separately where required.

Estimated Delivery Time

Total turnaround includes production and shipping time. For an event or fixed deadline, allow sufficient time for artwork approval, production and delivery.

Artwork Approval

Where a digital proof is required, production begins after artwork approval. Approval delays may affect the estimated delivery timeframe.

Bulk & Large Orders

Large corporate orders may require additional production and handling time. Delivery arrangements can vary based on shipment size and weight.

Delivery Address

Please ensure your delivery address, postal code and contact details are correct. Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delays.

Order Tracking

Where tracking is available, tracking information will be provided after dispatch.

Urgent Orders

If you require products by a specific date, please contact the ZenVibe team before ordering so production and delivery timing can be confirmed.

Delivery Delays

Delivery estimates may be affected by carrier delays, weather, public holidays, remote locations or circumstances outside normal production and shipping schedules.
