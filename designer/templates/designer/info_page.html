Your trusted Canadian partner for custom promotional products, corporate gifts, apparel, awards and event branding.

ZenVibe Corporate Gifts Inc. helps businesses, organizations, schools, sports teams and event planners create memorable brand experiences through high-quality custom promotional products and corporate gifts.

Based in Edmonton, Alberta, we proudly serve customers across Canada with custom apparel, awards and trophies, drinkware, banners and displays, marketing materials, corporate gift sets and promotional merchandise designed to increase brand visibility and leave a lasting impression.

Whether you need branded products for a trade show, employee recognition program, corporate event, sports tournament or marketing campaign, our team provides personalized service, quality products, competitive pricing and reliable nationwide delivery.
