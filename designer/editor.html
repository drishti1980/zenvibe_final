Quick tools

Printed text

Remove / Replace printed text

For flattened PNG/JPG or camera photos: drag a tight box over any printed name/date/text. ZenVibe removes the old raster lettering first, then creates a normal editable text layer. This is the reliable mode for script fonts and does not depend on OCR.

Document background

Changes the page colour. For flat PNG/JPG templates, ZenVibe also recolours the dominant paper/background area while preserving artwork and text as much as possible. The background artwork itself is selectable from Layers for crop, blur, resize and other image edits.

Colour presets

Artwork / floral colour

Select the uploaded artwork layer in Layers, then adjust the colourful parts of a flat PNG/JPG. Light paper and neutral black/grey text are preserved as much as possible.

Decorative artwork

For a flattened PNG/JPG, remove colourful floral/decorative pixels while keeping the light paper and dark text as much as possible. You can restore them at any time.
