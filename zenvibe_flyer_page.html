    Add and edit text

    Add and edit text

    Add custom text to your designs, or use text templates:

    1. Create or open an existing design.
    2. Click the Text tab from the side panel.
    3. Click Add a heading, Add a subheading, or Add a little bit of body text. You can also click a Typography to use.
    4. Double click on the text box added to your design to edit the text.
    5. Click outside the text box to finish.

    Click Duplicate from the context menu ( select text box → right click to show context menu ) to duplicate a selected text box, or click the Trash icon to delete it.

    Rotate and resize

    Rotate and resize

    Rotate and resize elements in your design:
    1. Create or open an existing design.
    2. Click the element, textbox, photo that you want to edit.
    3. To rotate, hold the rotating arrows handle and drag left or right. To resize, click any of the arrows  handles on the corners and drag in or out.
    4. Click outside of the selected element to finish.

    To have better control in resizing text, use the font size dropdown from the toolbar above the editor.

    Flip layers

    Flip layers

    Create or open an existing design, and refer below for flipping layers:

    1. Click to select the layers you want to flip.
    2. Right click to show context menu.
    3. Select Reflect Horizontal to flip it right side left, or Reflect Vertical to flip it upside down
    Layer, group, and align layers

    Layer, group, and align layers

    Create or open an existing design, and refer below to layer, group, or position your design layer s:

    Layer

    Arrange which layer sits on top of which:

    1. Click to select the layer  you want to move.
    2. Right Click to show context menu.
    3. Choose Bring Forward to move it up one layer, or Send To Back to move it down one layer.

    Group and ungroup

    Group layers to move or lock them together:

    1. Click and drag your cursor over the layers you want to group. You can also hold Shift on your keyboard, and click on multiple layers to select them.
    2. Click Group from the toolbar above the editor. You can also use CMD+G (Mac) or CTRL+G (Windows) on your keyboard.
    3. To ungroup, click to select the group, and click Ungroup from the toolbar above the editor. You can also use CTRL+Shift+G (Windows) on your keyboard.

    Align or position

    Align layers relative to the design page:

    1. Click to select the layer you want to align.
    2. Click Layer Position from the toolbar above the editor.
    3. Choose a position to apply it to the selected select .
    Add images

    Add images

    Add stock images to your designs from the Photos tab in the editor:

    1. Create or open an existing design.
    2. Click the Photos tab from the editor side panel.
    3. Click the images resource icons.
    4. Upload images or use the search bar to look for specific images.
    Crop images

    Crop images

    1. Click the image that you want to crop.
    2. Click Crop from the toolbar above the editor.
    3. Drag the white crop marks  to crop.
    4. Click on Crop to finish.
    Apply image shapes

    Apply image shapes

    Apply shapes to your images in a few clicks:

    1. Create or open an existing design.
    2. Select the photo you want to edit.
    3. Click Create clipping mask from the editor toolbar.
    4. Choose the shape you want in the dropdown to apply to your image.
    Upload requirements

    Upload requirements

    Make sure your files meet the upload requirements to use them in your designs.

    Only upload content that you own and are licensed to use.

    JPEG and PNG images

    • Under 10MB in size
    • Not more than 100 million total pixels (width x height)

    SVG images

    • Under 1MB in size
    • Saved with an SVG Profile of “SVG 1.1”
    • Expand all strokes, paths, and text boxes (Select your artwork > Object > Expand)
    • Group shapes with the same color together
    • Avoid using textures and gradients
    • Fit the artwork within the artboard (Object > Artboards > Fit to Selected Art)
    • Remove unnecessary layers, stray anchor points, and invisible objects
    • If you’re using Adobe Illustrator to create your SVG file, make sure to change the CSS Properties to Presentation Attributes before saving.
