Adding products — setup guide

Follow these steps, then save and check the product page.

  1. Product details: enter the product name, category and short summary.
  2. Product information: add the description, specifications, printing method, artwork requirements and FAQs. Use headings and bullet lists; no HTML is needed.
  3. Variants: add the sizes, shapes, materials and colours the product supports. Choose colours from the colour panel. Use unique keys such as navy-blue.
  4. Price tiers: set the base price and order limits. For quantity discounts, use non-overlapping Min/Max ranges, for example 20–49 and 50–99. Leave the last maximum blank for 100+.
  5. Images: upload a clear main image and optional gallery images; use labels and sort order to organise them.
  6. Delivery: enable the available delivery methods and enter their business days and charges.
  7. Editor & print areas: choose the editor mode, print dimensions in inches and editable pages. Check the template and printable area before making the product active.
  8. Visibility & sale: check availability and any sale price. Save, then preview the storefront and test its available options.

Configured shapes and materials appear as choices on the product page. The selected option is passed to the editor. Raw metadata is preserved in Editor & print areas and normally needs no manual changes.
